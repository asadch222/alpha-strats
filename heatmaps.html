How to read a heatmap

Size

The bigger the tile, the more money traded in that market over the last 24 hours. Big tiles are where the action is.

Colour

Green means the price is up over 24 hours, red means down. The brighter the colour, the bigger the move.

Groups

Markets are boxed together by type, such as chip makers or meme coins, so you can see when a whole sector moves at once.

What to look for

A screen that is mostly one colour shows a market moving together. A lone bright tile in a dull group is something unusual worth opening.
