Control room

Logged in as . Every switch saves straight away.

Open website
Preview mode. The login service is not connected yet, so what you change here is saved in this browser only. You will see it on the website in this browser; other visitors will not. Connect the login service (see config.js) and your changes reach everyone.

Open a trade

Pick a market, say which way you are trading and at what price. It appears on the home page with live profit and loss.

Your trades

Use the switch on each trade to show or hide it. Closing a trade locks in its result and moves it to the closed list on the website.

Pages

Switch a page off and it leaves the menu and every link. Anyone who opens it directly sees a "not available" message.

Sections

Switch off single parts of a page.

Announcement bar

A thin bar at the very top of every page. Good for news, offers or a new strategy.

Texts and prices

Leave a box empty to keep the original text.

Start again

Puts the website back exactly as it was built: everything shown, original texts, no trades.
